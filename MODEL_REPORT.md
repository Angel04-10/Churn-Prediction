# Churn model report

Generated by `python churn_model.py`. All selection decisions use 5-fold cross-validation
on the 3,600-customer training split; the 900-customer test split is only used for the
final numbers below.

The selected model at the default 0.50 threshold would catch only 57 churners
(recall 30%); lowering the threshold is what buys the extra true positives.
The threshold maximises the F2 score (recall weighted twice as much as precision) on
out-of-fold predictions.

Held-out ROC-AUC 0.762, average precision 0.513, Brier score 0.1369.

## What drives churn

Each input was shuffled 30 times on the held-out test set (together with every feature
engineered from it) and the drop in ROC-AUC measured; a bigger drop means the model relies
on it more. "Typical → changed" shows the model's probability for a typical customer
(11%) when only that input is changed.

| Rank | Feature | ROC-AUC drop when shuffled | Correlation with churn (all 4,500 customers) | Typical → changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Days Since Purchase | 0.182 | +0.27 | 15 → 10%, 75 → 26%, 120 → 36% |
| 2 | Subscription Status | 0.048 | -0.20 (has subscription) | None → 21%, Premium → 10% |
| 3 | Review Rating | 0.010 (within noise) | -0.07 | 2.3 → 16%, 4.7 → 6% |
| 4 | Return Frequency | 0.003 (within noise) | +0.04 | 0.02 → 9%, 0.38 → 12% |
| 5 | Metro | 0.001 (within noise) | +0.01 | — |
| 6 | Account Age Months | 0.001 (within noise) | -0.00 | — |

| 8 | Avg Purchase Amount INR | 0.000 (within noise) | +0.01 | — |
| 9 | Days Since Last Discount | 0.000 (within noise) | +0.01 | — |
| 10 | Discount Sensitivity | 0.000 (within noise) | -0.03 | — |
| 11 | Age | -0.000 (within noise) | -0.00 | — |
| 12 | Gender | -0.000 (within noise) | +0.01 | — |
| 13 | Purchase Frequency Per Month | -0.001 (within noise) | +0.01 | — |
| 14 | Customer Service Interactions | -0.001 (within noise) | -0.01 | — |

Days since purchase carries most of the signal (about three-quarters of it), then
subscription status. Review rating has a small effect: customers rating above 4.5 churn
less (14% vs. 23%), though on this 900-customer test set its importance is borderline.
Return frequency is weak (churn rises only from about 20% to 24% across its full 0–40%
range), and the remaining inputs have no measurable effect. "Within noise" means the
drop is smaller than two standard deviations across the 30 shuffles.
